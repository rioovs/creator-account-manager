Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Creator Account Manager (the "App") collects when you connect a TikTok account, how it is used, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

When you connect a TikTok account and grant permission, the App receives from TikTok only the data covered by the scopes you approve:

  • Basic profile (user.info.basic): open ID, display name, avatar.
  • Profile details (user.info.profile): username, bio, profile link, verified status.
  • Account statistics (user.info.stats): follower count, following count, likes count, video count.
  • Video list (video.list): your public videos' IDs, titles, cover images, links and statistics (views, likes, comments, shares).
  • Posting permission (video.upload, video.publish): permission to upload or publish videos you choose to your account.
  • Access tokens: OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok, used to call the API on your behalf.

We also store the videos, captions and scheduling settings you upload to the App for posting.

The App does not collect your TikTok password. Sign-in happens on TikTok's own pages.

2. How we use information

  • To display your accounts' profile information and performance dashboards.
  • To record statistics over time so trends can be shown in charts.
  • To upload and publish videos to your account when you instruct the App to.
  • To keep your connection active by refreshing access tokens.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties, except as needed to communicate with TikTok's API or when required by law.

3. Storage and security

Data is stored in the App's private database. Access tokens are encrypted at rest. Uploaded video files are kept only as long as needed to complete posting, and are then deleted. Only the App's operator has access to the dashboard.

4. Data retention and deletion

Data for a connected account is kept while the account stays connected. When you disconnect an account in the App, its tokens are deleted immediately and its stored profile data and statistics are deleted within 30 days. You can also revoke the App's access at any time from your TikTok account settings; the App will then no longer be able to access your data.

5. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the data the App holds about your TikTok account by contacting us (see below).

6. Children

The App is not intended for anyone under the minimum age required to use TikTok in their country.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it was last changed.

8. Contact

Privacy questions or data requests can be submitted through this project's GitHub issues page.

Creator Account Manager is not affiliated with or endorsed by TikTok.
